Israel is an apartheid state! End apartheid!

The imperialist-capitalist system divides the world into a rich Global North, which lives at the expense of the Global South. The countries of the Global South are willing to sell their raw materials, agricultural products and goods at a fair price, from which they could also live well. However, the labor and raw materials in the Global South should be exploited as cheaply as possible – for the profits of international corporations and banks. Raw materials, cheap goods and the necessary trade routes are therefore also secured with unfair contracts, counterinsurgency, repression and wars.


This unjust division of the world enables relative prosperity in the Global North, which is why many people here unfortunately accept this system, even though they know that billions suffer under it, that every 5 seconds a child dies of starvation. Democracy and security exist only in the Global North – prosperity makes it possible. For the Global South, there are authoritarian systems, oppression and war.


Nothing symbolizes the racist division of the world more than the apartheid wall in occupied Palestine. It separates the European Jewish colonial settlers from the indigenous Palestinian population: for some there is prosperity, privileges, security – for others military occupation, economic strangulation, human rights violations, prison camps, death penalty, displacement, ethnic cleansing, apartheid, genocide. What we know from the imperialist-capitalist system worldwide reaches its sharpest point in occupied Palestine. Here we can see most clearly where racism ultimately leads: namely to fascism.


From the Global South we only want cheap goods, good food, cheap vacations. People who want to come here, we lock out. They, at whose expense we live, should not participate in our prosperity. The walls and fences around Europe are getting higher and higher. Those who try to overcome them are shot at. Or we let them drown on their daring deadly crossings in the Mediterranean. Or we deport them. The misery and the wretched should be locked out. The Mediterranean connected people for centuries – now it has become a deadly trap thanks to the EU.


The fascistoid ideology, according to which certain lives are worth more and others less, is becoming popular again. One hundred thousand murdered Palestinians do not make the town hall light up in the colors of Palestine – remembrance is reserved exclusively for the Israeli victims. Right-wing ideology, such as excluding certain people from access to social benefits, finds broad approval among the population. Those who do not contribute should not receive anything either. We have seen this before in history. Those who do not share "our" values do not belong to us.


In times of crisis, the ruling class likes to play the piano of racist divisions. The government needs money for arms production, for war preparation. It gets the money by cutting social and health services. Many people are no longer confident that they will be able to afford their good life in the future. This suits the government very well, to incite hatred against minorities such as refugees and Muslims, to construct a "we" and "the others" towards whom one can feel superior.


Where this thinking ultimately leads, we have seen in the past under fascism and we see today in occupied Palestine – namely to genocide.
