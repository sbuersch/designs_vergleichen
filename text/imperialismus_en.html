Economic blackmail instead of free trade. The USA uses the global financial system and the dollar like a weapon. Anyone who does not submit or dares to use their own resources for the benefit of their own population is hit with sanctions that starve entire societies. It is not about "democracy," but about access to markets and raw materials for transnational corporations. This drive for constant expansion inevitably leads to conflict.


The military machine as the life insurance of capital. Over 800 US military bases worldwide do not serve our security. They are the strong-arm squad of a country that keeps the rest of the world dependent. And the EU happily joins in the beating. While we are told that rearmament serves peace, we have long known: Every euro that goes into tanks and rockets is a euro missing from education, health, and housing construction. We finance the wars of the elites with our own poverty.


Proxy wars on the backs of the peoples. Whether in Africa, Asia or South America – the pattern is always the same: The USA provokes conflicts far from their own borders to weaken competitors and cement their own supremacy. The price is paid by ordinary people, who are abused as cannon fodder for geopolitical chess games. We refuse to accept these strategic massacres as a "defense of values."


Cultural and media conformity. Imperialism is not only carried out with bombs, but also in the minds. Our media often act like the Pentagon's press office. Any country that strives for independent, sovereign development is immediately declared a "rogue state." Those who do not submit are hit with sanctions and bombs. We are supposed to believe that there is no alternative to Western supremacy. But true freedom means that every people can go its own way without having to ask permission from Washington.


Taking action against US imperialism is not a matter of ideology, but a matter of survival. If we want a world in which social justice and genuine peace are possible, we must first end the supremacy of the USA and its vassal Israel in West Asia. It is time for a politics that no longer lets itself be hitched to the wagon of a declining world power.
