More and more people are realizing: Things cannot continue the way politics is currently operating. Many problems are getting bigger instead of smaller – no matter which party is in power. This is not only due to individual decisions, but because the entire system is reaching its limits.

The problem with the system
Our economic system is based on the need to constantly produce more and generate more profit. Growth is mandatory – regardless of the consequences. At the same time, a large share of wealth is concentrated in the hands of a few people.

This leads to increasing exploitation of both people and the environment. Resources are becoming scarcer, inequality is growing – and conflicts are intensifying. When powerful states compete for influence and raw materials, tensions arise that repeatedly end in wars. The imperial West does not seek a peaceful world order with fair trade relations, but rather to maximize its profits at the expense of other peoples. Resistance against this form of neo-colonialism is suppressed with every available military means. While politicians keep us busy collecting plastic deposit bottles, the ruling elites destroy entire ecosystems with explosives, uranium, and other war materials. The production of weapons, their use in war, the destruction they cause, and the subsequent reconstruction are among the greatest environmental offenders!

Why “business as usual” is not a good solution
Nevertheless, many politicians claim that we can simply continue as before. But fewer and fewer people are convinced by this. Instead of offering real solutions, distracting fear campaigns and enemy images are being created. The new headscarf bans, for example, are nothing more than racist symbolic politics and an attempt to distract us from social cuts by dividing society.

The government’s course is becoming increasingly authoritarian: restrictions on freedom of dress, restrictions on freedom of speech. Even in the face of genocide in Gaza, the introduction of the death penalty exclusively for Palestinian prisoners, and the ongoing occupation, Graz city politics still upholds a resolution against the international civil society boycott movement BDS and against criticism of Zionism. Instead of condemning genocide in Gaza, they sanction criticism of Israel and solidarity with Palestine, thereby opening the door wide to repression.

Austria is supposed to become “fit for war” again
According to a report by SIPRI, global military spending reached a new all-time high last year – for the eleventh consecutive year. The Austrian government also wants to invest billions in rearmament, including the purchase of military equipment from what they describe as genocidal Israel. Companies in Styria are also planning to shift production toward military goods. The population’s military readiness is to be strengthened. Armament, the army, and extending military service are all meant to gain a positive image. We do not want to be driven blindly into another war. In Germany, young men already have to register if they want to leave the country for more than three months. It is time to wake up. The longer we continue down this path, the harder it will become to turn back.

Our voices against violence toward women
For decades, we have heard about programs to protect women from violence, and yet: every year dozens of women in Austria become victims of femicide, one in three women experiences physical and/or sexualized violence, more than half of all girls experience digital violence, the number of victims of sexual exploitation and trafficking of women continues to rise, as do cases of child abuse. Women and girls will only stop experiencing violence once we fundamentally change this system, because patriarchal structures are inseparably linked to capitalism. All the small machos look up to the big machos like Trump, Netanyahu, and Epstein, who demonstrate how to sexually exploit women with impunity, subjugate other peoples, bomb other countries in violation of international law, or torture and rape prisoners. Why then should ordinary individuals not also use violence to take what they supposedly deserve? If we do not put an end to warmongering, we fear an increase in violence against women and girls.

This system is making us sick!
With enormous systemic violence, globally unrestrained capitalism is destroying not only ecological and social systems, but also leaving devastation in people’s mentalities and psyches, especially among young people. In Austria, 370,000 people are considered alcohol dependent, around one million consume alcohol at levels harmful to their health, and 300,000 people suffer from gambling addiction. One quarter of all young people in Austria suffer from a mental illness, and around half say they do not feel well. 66% of Europeans report having experienced burnout or being close to it. Keep running faster in the hamster wheel, become better, prettier, fitter. Competition and self-optimization are the norm. Performance has to pay off. Those who cannot keep up are left behind. Tough luck. Endless streams of meaningless entertainment on television and social media. Less and less money for more and more work, lack of meaningful employment, environmental destruction, wars, and the feeling that politicians do not take people seriously. Resignation and hopelessness are spreading. And yet we still want to continue as before and only treat the individual? The conditions making people sick urgently need to be addressed.

What is going wrong?
1.7 million people in Austria are at risk of poverty! More than 170,000 more than the previous year. Particularly shocking is the extent of working poverty: for 361,000 people between the ages of 18 and 65, employment income is not enough to ensure a decent living. This includes many part-time workers, especially women, as well as workers in precarious jobs such as food delivery workers, harvest workers, and others. By the end of 2025, 269,500 people were completely unemployed. In addition, there is a considerable number of undocumented workers being exploited for the lowest wages without work permits. At the same time, the rich are becoming even richer: for the first time ever, there are 3,000 billionaires worldwide whose wealth has increased by 81% since 2020. Their combined fortune has now reached an astonishing 18.3 trillion dollars. On January 9th, top managers in Austria had already earned the equivalent of the average annual income of an employee. The gap between rich and poor continues to widen.

More and more people are being excluded from equal access to social benefits: people with subsidiary protection status, asylum seekers, and people who struggle to learn German at a higher level. If we do not resist this, step by step money will also be taken from other groups in society, as we can already see in the planned increase of unemployment contributions for low-income earners. “Saving money” is the wrong word for this, because apparently there is enough money available – it is simply being redistributed elsewhere, for example into the military.

We live in a global world
Our daily lives have long been connected with the entire world – something everyone understood at the latest after the closure of the Strait of Hormuz. Our clothes are mostly produced under very poor conditions, the imperial West wants to obtain the raw materials necessary for our prosperity as cheaply as possible or even seize them by force, and our consumption affects the climate and environment worldwide.

At the same time, we in the Global North benefit from structures that disadvantage other countries and drive them into poverty. Many people are therefore forced to leave their homelands – not voluntarily, but because they no longer have any prospects. These problems cannot be solved simply by closing borders.

Why Gaza?
Gaza represents the brutal escalation of a world order in which the Global North exploits and kills the rest of the world while presenting itself as a moral authority. But Gaza also stands above all for the hope of finally putting an end to this once and for all. The atrocities committed by the USA and Israel have awakened millions – people who previously had nothing to do with politics or protests took to the streets loudly demanding freedom, peace, and justice. As part of this movement, we want to contribute to overcoming capitalism, a system that carries war within it as the cloud carries the rain.

Change must begin everywhere – not only at the EU or federal level, but also locally. The mayor of Genoa, for example, showed solidarity with the dockworkers of her city who went on strike against loading a ship with weapons bound for Israel. In 2023, Barcelona ended its twin-city partnership with Tel Aviv, froze all relations with Israel, and called for a boycott of the apartheid state. The city administration of Oslo prohibited trade in goods from Israeli settlements. These examples show: another way is possible!

That is why the list GAZA – Voices for Global Justice is running in the 2026 Graz municipal elections.
